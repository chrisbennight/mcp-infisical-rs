# Conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss the work without personal attacks,
harassment, discrimination, threats, or publication of private information.
Explain disagreements with evidence and leave room for people to correct mistakes.

The repository owner, [chrisbennight](https://github.com/chrisbennight), handles
conduct reports and may remove content or restrict participation. To request a
private reporting channel, open an issue tagging the owner and state only that
you need a private conduct contact. Do not name affected people or publish the
incident details. The owner must establish a private channel before collecting
those details. There is no guaranteed response time.
